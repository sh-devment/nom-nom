## nom-nom

A calorie tracker.

I decided to lose weight and built a calorie tracking app for it.
Looking up calories by hand is a chore, so an AI analyses the food instead.

## What's inside

- **Daily calorie log** — everything you eat in a day, in one place
- **Macros from a photo or a description** — snap your meal or describe it in words
- **Labels work too** — shoot the nutrition facts on the package, not just the food
- **Weight** — log changes and track your progress
